# lynx_assignment

Here is a highlight of the findings included in the Yellow_Taxi.ipynb Jupyter notebook.

## Payment Types
- Cash is declining
- Flex Fares eat into Credit Card’s share of Payment, but Credit Card’s share increases again from 2020 onwards

## Fare Amount and Distance
- Strong correlation between Fare Amount and Trip distance: unsurprising
- There is a vertical line at Fare Amount = `$52`. After further Analysis this turns out to be 98.7% JFK trips and the rest coincidentally costing `$52`.

## Hourly Pick-up Patterns by Year
- In 2018 and 2019 there is little change in daily pick-up patterns
- In 2020 and 2021 there is a change towards the middle of the day with notable reductions at night
- In 2022 the distribution looks more like pre-covid although fewer trips during the night

## Trip Profile
- Trip Distance, Duration and Total Trip Amounts heavily skew left
- Most Trips are short and cost a few dollars

## Vendor ridership and revenue
- In total ridership has not recovered to per-covid levels
- Creative Mobile Tech saw a decrease of 24% in revenue from 2018 to 2022 (2018: 316204, 2022: 240684)
- Ridership for Creative Mobility dropped 32% from 2018 to 2022 (2018: 24877, 2022: 17007)
- Curb Mobility saw an increase of 37% in revenue from 2018 to 2022 (2018: 467687, 2022: 640886)
- Ridership for Curb Mobility increased by 23% from 2018 to 2022 (2018: 34804, 2022: 42888)
- Curb Mobility seems to have strengthened it's position even though Taxi Ridership is declining
- Both companies have increased their revenue per ride since revenue either decreased less or increased more compared to ridership

## High Revenue Pick-up Locations
- Of the top 10 pick-up locations 9 are also high value destinations. This is interesting because these locations are two-sided taxi demand hubs. People frequently seem to travel from and to that area.
